# Chicken Enchiladas

A split intermediate: two thirds of the sauce goes into the filling, the rest over the top.

Makes 4 servings · 20 min hands-on, 50 min start to finish

## Ingredients

- 400 g chopped tomatoes
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 200 g chicken thigh, diced
- 1 onion, sliced
- 4 flour tortillas
- 100 g cheddar, grated

## Steps

1. Simmer 400 g chopped tomatoes with 1 tbsp chili powder until thick, about 15 minutes.
2. Fry 200 g chicken thigh and 1 onion until the chicken is cooked through, about 8 minutes.
3. Stir ⅔ of the tomato sauce into the fried chicken and onion and simmer for 5 minutes.
4. Divide the filling between 4 flour tortillas, roll them up and lay them seam side down in a baking dish.
5. Pour the remaining tomato sauce over the rolled tortillas and scatter with 100 g cheddar.
6. Bake the covered enchiladas at 400°F (205°C) until golden, about 20 minutes.
