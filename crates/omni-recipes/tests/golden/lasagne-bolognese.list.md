# Lasagne alla Bolognese

Slow-cooked ragù and béchamel layered with fresh pasta. The ragù is better made a day ahead.

Makes 8 servings · 65 min hands-on, 4 h 25 min start to finish

## Ingredients

**Ragù** (make ahead: keeps 3 days in the fridge)

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 yellow onion, finely chopped
- 1 carrot, finely chopped
- 1 stalk celery, finely chopped
- 500 g ground beef
- 250 g ground pork
- kosher salt, to taste
- 180 ml dry white wine
- 240 ml whole milk
- 700 g tomato passata
- 2 tbsp tomato paste

**Béchamel**

- 60 g unsalted butter
- 60 g all-purpose flour
- 750 ml whole milk, warmed
- 1 pinch nutmeg, freshly grated
- kosher salt, to taste

**Assembly**

- 1 tbsp butter (for the dish)
- 250 g fresh lasagne sheets
- 100 g Parmigiano Reggiano, finely grated, divided
- Ragù (from above)
- Béchamel (from above)

## Steps

**Ragù**

1. Warm 2 tbsp olive oil in a Dutch oven over medium-low heat. Cook 1 yellow onion, 1 carrot and 1 stalk celery until soft but not browned, about 10 minutes.
2. Raise the heat to medium-high. Add 500 g ground beef and 250 g ground pork to the soffritto, season with kosher salt, and cook, breaking the meat up, until it has lost its pink colour and starts to brown, about 10 minutes.
3. Pour 180 ml dry white wine over the browned meat and boil until the pan is nearly dry, about 5 minutes.
4. Add 240 ml whole milk to the meat base and simmer until it has been absorbed, about 10 minutes.
5. Stir 700 g tomato passata and 2 tbsp tomato paste into the milk-braised meat. Cover with the lid ajar and simmer on the lowest heat for 2.5 hours, stirring now and then, until thick and glossy.

**Béchamel**

6. Melt 60 g unsalted butter in a saucepan over medium heat, whisk in 60 g all-purpose flour and cook for 2 minutes without colouring.
7. Whisk 750 ml whole milk into the roux a ladle at a time. Simmer, whisking, until it coats the back of a spoon, 8–10 minutes.
8. Season the white sauce with 1 pinch nutmeg and kosher salt.

**Assembly**

9. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
10. Grease a 23 x 33 cm baking dish with 1 tbsp butter. Using all of the ragù, 250 g fresh lasagne sheets, ¾ of the béchamel and 70 g Parmigiano Reggiano, build four layers: a thin coat of ragù, a sheet of pasta, a few spoonfuls of béchamel and a handful of cheese.
11. Spread the remaining béchamel over the top and finish with the remaining 30 g Parmigiano Reggiano.
12. Cover the topped lasagne with foil and bake in the hot oven for 25 minutes.
13. Remove the foil from the lasagne and bake 15–20 minutes more, until browned and bubbling at the edges.
14. Rest the browned lasagne for 15 minutes before cutting.
