# Chicken Piccata

Pan-seared cutlets in a lemon, caper and butter pan sauce.

Makes 4 servings · 25 min hands-on, 25 min start to finish

## Ingredients

- 500 g boneless, skinless chicken breasts, halved horizontally into cutlets
- kosher salt, to taste
- black pepper, to taste
- 60 g (½ cup) all-purpose flour (for dredging)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 4 tbsp unsalted butter, cold, divided
- 120 ml (½ cup) dry white wine
- 180 ml (¾ cup) chicken stock
- 2 lemons, divided
- 2 tbsp capers, drained (optional)
- 2 tbsp flat-leaf parsley, chopped

## Steps

1. Season 500 g boneless, skinless chicken breasts on both sides with kosher salt and black pepper. Dredge in 60 g all-purpose flour and shake off the excess.
2. Heat 2 tbsp olive oil and 2 tbsp unsalted butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat until the butter foams. Cook the dredged cutlets until golden, 3 to 4 minutes per side, then move to a plate. Keep the pan on the heat.
3. Pour 120 ml dry white wine into the hot pan and scrape up the browned bits. Boil until reduced by half, about 2 minutes.
4. Add 180 ml chicken stock, the juice of 1 lemon and 2 tbsp capers to the wine reduction. Simmer until reduced by about half, 4–5 minutes.
5. Take the sauce base off the heat and swirl in the remaining 2 tbsp unsalted butter a piece at a time. Taste and adjust with kosher salt.
6. Return the seared cutlets to the pan and spoon the lemon butter sauce over them to warm through, about 1 minute. Scatter with 2 tbsp flat-leaf parsley and serve with 1 lemon, cut into wedges.
