# Shortbread rounds

Buttery rounds cut from chilled dough, with a thin, dry hardtack-style version that keeps for weeks.

Makes 72 rounds (scaled ×2) · 15 min hands-on, 5 h 55 min start to finish

## Ingredients

- 450 g (2 cups) unsalted butter, softened
- 200 g (1 cup) granulated sugar
- 600 g all-purpose flour
- ½ tsp fine sea salt

## Steps

1. Heat the oven and line two baking sheets with parchment.
2. Beat 450 g unsalted butter with 200 g granulated sugar until pale and soft, 3 minutes.
3. Mix 600 g all-purpose flour and ½ tsp fine sea salt into the creamed butter until it just comes together.
4. Flatten the dough into a disc, wrap it and chill for 30 minutes.
5. Roll the chilled dough 3–4 mm (⅛ in) thick and cut 72 rounds with a 6 cm (2½ in) cutter, rerolling the scraps once.
6. Bake the rounds in the hot oven in 6 batches at 170°C (340°F) until pale gold, then lower the oven to 120°C (250°F) and dry them out, 40–50 minutes each in all.
7. Cool the baked rounds on the sheet for 10 minutes, then on a rack.
