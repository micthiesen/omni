# Shortbread rounds

Buttery rounds cut from chilled dough, with a thin, dry hardtack-style version that keeps for weeks.

Makes 24 rounds · 15 min hands-on, 1 h 30 min start to finish

## Ingredients

- 225 g (1 cup) unsalted butter, softened
- 100 g (½ cup) granulated sugar
- 300 g all-purpose flour
- ¼ tsp fine sea salt

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 170°C (340°F) and line two baking sheets with parchment.
2. Beat 225 g unsalted butter with 100 g granulated sugar until pale and soft, 3 minutes.
3. Mix 300 g all-purpose flour and ¼ tsp fine sea salt into the creamed butter until it just comes together.
4. Flatten the dough into a disc, wrap it and chill for 30 minutes.
5. Roll the chilled dough 6–7 mm (¼ in) thick and cut 24 rounds with a 6 cm (2½ in) cutter, rerolling the scraps once.
6. Bake the rounds in the hot oven in 2 batches, one sheet at a time, 14–16 minutes each.
7. Cool the baked rounds on the sheet for 10 minutes, then on a rack.
