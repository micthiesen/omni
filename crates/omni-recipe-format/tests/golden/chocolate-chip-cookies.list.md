# Chocolate Chip Cookies

Crisp-edged, soft-centred cookies from a creamed-butter dough.

Makes 28 cookies · 20 min hands-on, 35 min start to finish

## Ingredients

- 280 g (2¼ cups) all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp fine sea salt
- 225 g (1 cup) unsalted butter, softened
- 150 g (¾ cup) granulated sugar
- 165 g (¾ cup) light brown sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 340 g (2 cups) semisweet chocolate chips
- 100 g walnuts, chopped (optional)
- flaky salt, to taste (optional)

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C) with racks in the upper and lower thirds. Line two baking sheets with parchment.
2. Whisk 280 g all-purpose flour, 1 tsp baking soda and 1 tsp fine sea salt together in a bowl.
3. Beat 225 g unsalted butter with 150 g granulated sugar and 165 g light brown sugar until pale and fluffy, 3–4 minutes.
4. Beat 2 large eggs into the creamed butter one at a time, then 2 tsp vanilla extract.
5. Add the dry mix to the wet mix and mix on low just until no dry flour remains.
6. Fold 340 g semisweet chocolate chips and, if using, 100 g walnuts into the dough.
7. Scoop the cookie dough into 45 g balls and space them 5 cm apart on the lined sheets.
8. Bake the scooped dough in the hot oven for 9–11 minutes, swapping the sheets halfway, until the edges are golden and the centres still look soft.
9. Sprinkle the baked cookies with flaky salt if you like. Cool on the sheets for 5 minutes, then move to a rack.
