# Chocolate Chip Cookies

Crisp-edged, soft-centred cookies from a creamed-butter dough.

Makes 28 cookies · 20 min hands-on, 35 min start to finish

## Ingredients

- 280 g (2¼ cups) all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp fine sea salt
- 225 g (1 cup) unsalted butter, softened
- 150 g (¾ cup) granulated sugar
- 165 g (¾ cup) light brown sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 340 g (2 cups) semisweet chocolate chips
- 100 g walnuts, chopped (optional)
- flaky salt, to taste (optional)

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C) with racks in the upper and lower thirds. Line two baking sheets with parchment.
2. Whisk 280 g all-purpose flour, 1 tsp baking soda and 1 tsp fine sea salt together in a bowl.
3. Beat 225 g unsalted butter with 150 g granulated sugar and 165 g light brown sugar until pale and fluffy, 3–4 minutes.
4. Beat 2 large eggs into the creamed butter one at a time, then 2 tsp vanilla extract.
5. Add the dry mix to the wet mix and mix on low just until no dry flour remains.
6. Fold 340 g semisweet chocolate chips and, if using, 100 g walnuts into the dough.
7. Scoop the cookie dough into 45 g balls and space them 5 cm apart on the lined sheets.
8. Bake the scooped dough in the hot oven for 9–11 minutes, swapping the sheets halfway, until the edges are golden and the centres still look soft.
9. Sprinkle the baked cookies with flaky salt if you like. Cool on the sheets for 5 minutes, then move to a rack.

# Chicken Piccata

Pan-seared cutlets in a lemon, caper and butter pan sauce.

Makes 4 servings · 25 min hands-on, 25 min start to finish

## Ingredients

- 500 g boneless, skinless chicken breasts, halved horizontally into cutlets
- kosher salt, to taste
- black pepper, to taste
- 60 g (½ cup) all-purpose flour (for dredging)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 4 tbsp unsalted butter, cold, divided
- 120 ml (½ cup) dry white wine
- 180 ml (¾ cup) chicken stock
- 2 lemons, divided
- 2 tbsp capers, drained (optional)
- 2 tbsp flat-leaf parsley, chopped

## Steps

1. Season 500 g boneless, skinless chicken breasts on both sides with kosher salt and black pepper. Dredge in 60 g all-purpose flour and shake off the excess.
2. Heat 2 tbsp olive oil and 2 tbsp unsalted butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat until the butter foams. Cook the dredged cutlets until golden, 3 to 4 minutes per side, then move to a plate. Keep the pan on the heat.
3. Pour 120 ml dry white wine into the hot pan and scrape up the browned bits. Boil until reduced by half, about 2 minutes.
4. Add 180 ml chicken stock, the juice of 1 lemon and 2 tbsp capers to the wine reduction. Simmer until reduced by about half, 4–5 minutes.
5. Take the sauce base off the heat and swirl in the remaining 2 tbsp unsalted butter a piece at a time. Taste and adjust with kosher salt.
6. Return the seared cutlets to the pan and spoon the lemon butter sauce over them to warm through, about 1 minute. Scatter with 2 tbsp flat-leaf parsley and serve with 1 lemon, cut into wedges.

# Lasagne alla Bolognese

Slow-cooked ragù and béchamel layered with fresh pasta. The ragù is better made a day ahead.

Makes 8 servings · 65 min hands-on, 4 h 25 min start to finish

## Ingredients

**Ragù** (make ahead: keeps 3 days in the fridge)

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 yellow onion, finely chopped
- 1 carrot, finely chopped
- 1 stalk celery, finely chopped
- 500 g ground beef
- 250 g ground pork
- kosher salt, to taste
- 180 ml dry white wine
- 240 ml whole milk
- 700 g tomato passata
- 2 tbsp tomato paste

**Béchamel**

- 60 g unsalted butter
- 60 g all-purpose flour
- 750 ml whole milk, warmed
- 1 pinch nutmeg, freshly grated
- kosher salt, to taste

**Assembly**

- 1 tbsp butter (for the dish)
- 250 g fresh lasagne sheets
- 100 g Parmigiano Reggiano, finely grated, divided
- Ragù (from above)
- Béchamel (from above)

## Steps

**Ragù**

1. Warm 2 tbsp olive oil in a Dutch oven over medium-low heat. Cook 1 yellow onion, 1 carrot and 1 stalk celery until soft but not browned, about 10 minutes.
2. Raise the heat to medium-high. Add 500 g ground beef and 250 g ground pork to the soffritto, season with kosher salt, and cook, breaking the meat up, until it has lost its pink colour and starts to brown, about 10 minutes.
3. Pour 180 ml dry white wine over the browned meat and boil until the pan is nearly dry, about 5 minutes.
4. Add 240 ml whole milk to the meat base and simmer until it has been absorbed, about 10 minutes.
5. Stir 700 g tomato passata and 2 tbsp tomato paste into the milk-braised meat. Cover with the lid ajar and simmer on the lowest heat for 2.5 hours, stirring now and then, until thick and glossy.

**Béchamel**

6. Melt 60 g unsalted butter in a saucepan over medium heat, whisk in 60 g all-purpose flour and cook for 2 minutes without colouring.
7. Whisk 750 ml whole milk into the roux a ladle at a time. Simmer, whisking, until it coats the back of a spoon, 8–10 minutes.
8. Season the white sauce with 1 pinch nutmeg and kosher salt.

**Assembly**

9. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
10. Grease a 23 x 33 cm baking dish with 1 tbsp butter. Using all of the ragù, 250 g fresh lasagne sheets, ¾ of the béchamel and 70 g Parmigiano Reggiano, build four layers: a thin coat of ragù, a sheet of pasta, a few spoonfuls of béchamel and a handful of cheese.
11. Spread the remaining béchamel over the top and finish with the remaining 30 g Parmigiano Reggiano.
12. Cover the topped lasagne with foil and bake in the hot oven for 25 minutes.
13. Remove the foil from the lasagne and bake 15–20 minutes more, until browned and bubbling at the edges.
14. Rest the browned lasagne for 15 minutes before cutting.

# Chicken Enchiladas

A split intermediate: two thirds of the sauce goes into the filling, the rest over the top.

Makes 4 servings · 20 min hands-on, 50 min start to finish

## Ingredients

- 400 g chopped tomatoes
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 200 g chicken thigh, diced
- 1 onion, sliced
- 4 flour tortillas
- 100 g cheddar, grated

## Steps

1. Simmer 400 g chopped tomatoes with 1 tbsp chili powder until thick, about 15 minutes.
2. Fry 200 g chicken thigh and 1 onion until the chicken is cooked through, about 8 minutes.
3. Stir ⅔ of the tomato sauce into the fried chicken and onion and simmer for 5 minutes.
4. Divide the filling between 4 flour tortillas, roll them up and lay them seam side down in a baking dish.
5. Pour the remaining tomato sauce over the rolled tortillas and scatter with 100 g cheddar.
6. Bake the covered enchiladas at 400°F (205°C) until golden, about 20 minutes.
